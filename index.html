About

Our mission is to advance the responsible, human-centred use of technology by placing ethics and governance at the heart of research and practice. We bring together scholars, practitioners and students to study how artificial intelligence and digital systems are reshaping organisations, education, healthcare and society, and to turn that evidence into practical guidance for decision-makers.

Rigorous research

Producing evidence-based, interdisciplinary studies on ethics, accountability and AI governance.

Responsible innovation

Supporting trustworthy, fair and transparent adoption of AI in workplaces, classrooms and care settings.

Open collaboration

Connecting researchers and institutions across disciplines and borders to share methods, data and ideas.

Knowledge for impact

Translating findings into guidance for educators, HR leaders, policymakers and practitioners.

How a collaboration runs

    Submit a short idea and your intended role.
  2. 2Match
    We connect you with researchers who share the question.
  3. 3Protocol
    Agree scope, roles, authorship, and data terms in writing.
  4. 4Publish
    Pre-register where suitable and share outputs openly.

Working principles

Shared commitments that keep collaboration fair, rigorous, and useful.

Transparent methods
Reporting follows recognised guidance such as PRISMA for reviews, with search strategies and screening decisions documented.
Fair authorship
Contributions and credit are agreed at the start and revisited as the work evolves.
Inclusive perspectives
We invite varied disciplines, regions, and cultural and religious viewpoints on technology and responsibility.
Research integrity
Ethics approval, competing-interest and funding disclosures, and responsible data handling are expected in every project.
Open where possible
Prefer open-access venues without article processing charges and openly shared materials.
Practical impact
Findings are written up for both scholarly audiences and decision-makers.
